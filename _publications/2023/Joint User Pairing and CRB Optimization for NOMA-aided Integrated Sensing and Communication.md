abstract: >-
  Abstract—Integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) 
  is a promising paradigm to efficiently utilize the radio 
  resources and hardware for both sensing and communication 
  in the future Internet of Things (IoT). In this paper, we 
  investigate a non-orthogonal multiple access (NOMA)-aided 
  ISAC system in which the ISAC base station (BS) serves multiple 
  pairs of NOMA users with the same spectrum resources while performing 
  target estimation by utilizing the superimposed signal. 
  To investigate this problem, we formulate a joint optimization 
  of the user pairing and the beamforming vectors for all NOMA users, 
  with the objective of minimizing the Cramer-Rao bound (CRB) for sensing. 
  Despite that the formulated problem is strictly non-convex, we adopt the successive 
  convex approximation and semidefinite relaxation to transform the problem into a 
  tractable form and propose an efficient algorithm for solving it. Numerical 
  results demonstrate the performance gains achieved by our NOMA-aided ISAC 
  with the user pairing scheme in the aspects of sensing performance, 
  spectral efficiency for transmission and energy efficiency for transmission.
